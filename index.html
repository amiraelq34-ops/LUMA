Questions

Is LUMA free?

Yes. During the private beta LUMA is completely free.

Do I need to download an app?

No. LUMA opens in your phone's browser. You can add it to your home screen so it feels like an app.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing breaks. LUMA counts the days you showed up this moon, and each new moon is a fresh start.

Is this medical advice?

No. LUMA is for everyday wellbeing and reflection. For health concerns, please speak to a doctor.

Who is LUMA for?

Women 18 and over who want a calmer, more intentional rhythm to their days.

No streaks to break. Just the days you showed up for yourself.
